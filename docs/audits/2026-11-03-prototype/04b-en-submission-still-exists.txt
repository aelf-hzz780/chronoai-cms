## Identity is the starting point

As agents move from answering questions to getting things done, identity becomes part of the workflow — not just the sign-in screen.

A connected identity gives every action the context it needs:

- A clear owner and a verifiable permission
- The right account for the right destination
- A meaningful confirmation before publishing
